INTRODUCTION
- - - - - - - - - - - - - - -

Calculus Facts Tour is a C#-oriented 2D educational platformer created in Unity. The game is designed to introduce players to important Calculus 1 and Calculus 2 formulas while they play through a series of platforming levels.

The main purpose of the game is to help people become familiar with formulas from different Calculus modules before taking Calculus 1 or 2. Calculus formulas and lesson information are displayed in the background throughout the game so that players can read and recognize them while completing each level.

There are a total of 4 levels. Each level represents a different Calculus chapter or lesson and contains formulas and information related to that topic. Players must jump across platforms and reach the end of each level in order to continue.

The player is limited to 2 jumps while in the air. However, the player can jump from the sides of walls and platforms multiple times, allowing for repeated wall jumps. This makes wall jumping an important part of moving through some sections of the game.

The player cannot permanently die in Calculus Facts Tour. If the player touches a spike, the player is sent back to the start of the current level. If the player falls off the level or runs out of time, the game restarts from the beginning of Level 1 / Chapter 1.

The timer resets every time the player enters a new level, giving the player a new amount of time to complete that level.

After completing the game, the player is given a 4-question mini quiz. The quiz is based on the Calculus information and formulas shown during the game. By paying attention to the formulas while playing, the player can gain more knowledge about Calculus and have a better idea of what they may learn in a future Calculus 1 or Calculus 2 class.


GAMEPLAY RULES
- - - - - - - - - - - - - - -

1. There are 4 total levels in the game.
2. Each level contains Calculus 1/2 formulas and lesson information in the background.
3. Players must jump across platforms to reach the end of each level.
4. The player can jump up to 2 times while in the air.
5. The player can perform repeated wall jumps by jumping from the sides of walls or platforms.
6. Spikes can harm the player and send the player back to the beginning of the current level.
7. Falling off the level sends the player back to Level 1 / Chapter 1.
8. Running out of time also sends the player back to Level 1 / Chapter 1.
9. The timer resets whenever the player enters a new level.
10. The player cannot permanently die in the game.
11. A 4-question mini quiz appears after the game to test what the player learned from the Calculus formulas and information shown throughout the levels.

QUESTIONS
- - - - - - - - - - - - - - -

Q: How is this game educational?
A: Calculus Facts Tour displays Calculus 1 and Calculus 2 formulas and lesson information in the background while the player completes each level. Each level focuses on information from a different Calculus chapter or module. The goal is to help players become familiar with Calculus concepts before taking the class.

Q: What is the main purpose of the game?
A: The main purpose is to introduce players to formulas and topics they may see in Calculus AB or Calculus BC. The platforming gameplay keeps the player active while the educational information remains visible throughout the levels.

Q: How many levels are in the game?
A: There are 4 levels. Each level includes different Calculus formulas or lesson information.

Q: Is there a quiz?
A: Yes. There is a 4-question mini quiz after the game. Paying attention to the formulas and information displayed during the levels can help the player answer the questions.

Q: Can the player die?
A: The player cannot permanently die. Touching a spike sends the player back to the start of the current level. Falling or running out of time restarts the game from Level 1 / Chapter 1.

Q: How does jumping work?
A: The player is limited to 2 jumps while in the air. However, the player can jump from the sides of walls and platforms multiple times, allowing repeated wall jumps.

Q: What happens when the timer reaches zero?
A: If the timer reaches zero, the player is returned to Level 1 / Chapter 1. The timer resets each time the player enters a new level.

Q: Do I need a mouse to play the game?
A: The keyboard is used for the main gameplay controls. A mouse may be used when selecting menu buttons.

Q: Where is the code for the game?
A: The code and scripts used for the game are stored in the Unity project Assets folder, including the levels, sprites, and C# scripts used to create the game.

SOFTWARE USED
- - - - - - - - - - - - - - -

Unity was the main game engine used to develop Calculus Facts Tour.

C# was used to program the game's mechanics, player movement, level behavior, timer system, hazards, wall jumping, quiz features, and other gameplay functions.

Microsoft Visual Studio 2019 was used as the development environment for writing and editing the C# scripts used in the Unity project.

Adobe Photoshop was used for creating or editing visual assets such as platforms, sprites, backgrounds, and other 2D game elements.
